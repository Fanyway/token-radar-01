How the value is priced

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

Category multiple

Median market-cap-to-TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category, read from this snapshot. A category needs at least five live protocols before its own median is trusted; thinner categories fall back to the global median. Clamped to 0.05 – 1.50.

Growth multiplier

1 + 0.75 × the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60% … +150%. Fast-growing TVL is worth more than shrinking TVL, but one week of flow is not the whole story.

Maturity multiplier

0.70 + 0.30 × age ÷ 3 years. Older protocols are worth more than a two-day-old pool. A protocol with no listing date is treated as one year old.
